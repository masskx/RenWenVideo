标题：Governor Kotek Pauses Work on Requests for State Land to Support New Data Centers

要点摘录：
Today, Governor Tina Kotek directed every state agency to immediately pause unapproved requests for easements, rights-of-way, leases, rentals, land-use permits, and sales or transfers of state-owned property for data center projects. The pause remains in effect through July 1, 2027, or until statewide action from the Governor’s Office or the Legislature directs otherwise.

“Data center corporations are asking communities to absorb enormous demands on our water, energy, infrastructure, and natural resources..."

While the state currently lacks authority to enact a statewide moratorium on data center development, this action reflects the Governor’s commitment to taking steps within the state’s control immediately.

Governor Kotek signed the Protecting Oregonians With Energy Responsibility (POWER) Act in 2025 ... already resulted in a 29% increase in electricity rates on data center corporations and rate decreases for all other ratepayers served by Portland General Electric.
