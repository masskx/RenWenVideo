    Dear Governor Abbott:

    Thank you for your letter dated September 21, 2026, directing the Texas Commission on
    Environmental Quality (TCEQ) to pause the issuance of permits related to data center projects.

    Per your request, this letter provides an update on agency activities to comply with your
    directive. TCEQ has paused the issuance of all permits related to the construction or
    development of infrastructure directly supporting data centers.

    TCEQ staff are screening pending permit applications to determine if they are subject to the
    directive. If a permit application is subject to the directive, TCEQ has paused that permit
    application in accordance with your directive. In addition, TCEQ is screening claimed
    authorizations that do not require the agency to affirmatively issue a permit and, where
    possible, is suspending such authorizations for data center activities.

    TCEQ has met with the Texas Water Development Board (TWDB), the Public Utility Commission
    of Texas (PUCT), and the Electric Reliability Council of Texas (ERCOT) to ensure that all agency
    directives are aligned. We have also provided TCEQ resource tools to other state agencies to
    obtain real-time data on the status of all pending and issued permits.

    TCEQ is committed to handling permits according to your directive and continues to operate in
    a manner that ensures we protect our state's public health and natural resources consistent
    with sustainable economic development.
