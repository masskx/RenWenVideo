ChatGPT can now create GIFs using a prompt or a photo uploaded to the AI chatbot. The update, which is rolling out alongside OpenAI’s new Images 2.6 model, allows you to adjust the GIF’s animation speed, edit individual frames, and share it with others.

Before this change, ChatGPT couldn’t natively generate GIFs in a single step. An example shared by the company shows how ChatGPT can generate a GIF in response to the prompt, “can you make a gif of a cat dancing?” while also providing you with the option to change the playback speed and view it frame by frame.

[![Image 8: You can adjust speed and edit frames directly in ChatGPT.](https://platform.theverge.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2026/11/GIF.png?quality=90&strip=all&crop=0%2C0%2C100%2C100&w=2400)](https://platform.theverge.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/2/2026/11/GIF.png?quality=90&strip=all&crop=0,0,100,100)

_You can adjust speed and edit frames directly in ChatGPT._

Image: OpenAI

Along with support for GIF-making, the Images 2.6 update also enables faster image generation in ChatGPT, OpenAI says. The company says users now generate over 4 billion images per week across ChatGPT and its API, a jump from the 3 billion per week [it reported in September](https://openai.com/index/introducing-chatgpt-images-2-5/).
