- WSJ 独家可见标题为 *Exclusive | Nvidia Is Preparing to Oust CEO Jensen Huang*。页面显示 Updated Nov. 11, 2026 7:17 pm ET（台北时间 11-12 08:17）。JSON 元数据里还有 publishedDateTimeUtc `2026-11-11T22:28:00Z`（台北时间 11-12 06:28）。正文在付费墙后，**未绕墙、未引用付费正文**。
- 韩国《中央日报》英文版 Korea Times 转载的路透稿写：Nvidia's board is meeting late Wednesday to decide whether to oust CEO Jensen Huang, a source familiar with the matter told Reuters。并写 Nvidia did not immediately respond to a request for comment on the board meeting。

### 5. 关键人风险／直接下属人数：官方文件与媒体数字不是同一句话

- **FY2026 10-K（公司年报，已核）：** “Additionally, we are highly dependent on the services of our longstanding executive team. Failure to ensure effective succession planning, transfer of knowledge, and smooth transitions involving executives and key employees could hinder our strategic planning, execution, and long-term success.” 这是公司自己披露的依赖与继任规划风险，可标「公司年报」。它**没有**点名继任者，也**没有**写直接下属人数。
- **2026 年代理声明（已核）：** “We believe in extensive leadership development. Our internal leadership structure ensures that many senior managers work directly with our CEO to execute our corporate strategies. … and presents the Board with a pool of excellent candidates for future promotion.” 只写 many senior managers，**不再给数字**。
- **2024 年代理声明（已核原句）：** “Our management structure ensures that over 60 senior managers work directly with our CEO to execute our corporate strategies.” 这是「超过 60 名高管直接与 CEO 共事」的官方旧表述。Ars Technica／TechCrunch 所说 60，应追溯到这类 2024 年披露或媒体转述，**不要当成 2026 年仍在文件里的现行数字**。
- **每经写「33 名直接下属」：** 未在 FY2026 10-K 或 2026 年代理声明中核到，**不要用**。
- 路透写 “dozens of direct reports” 和 “no obvious successor”，是媒体概括。成片宜用：「公司年报写高度依赖长期经营团队，并提醒继任规划风险；2026 年治理文件未点名继任者。2024 年文件曾写有逾 60 名高管直接与 CEO 共事，2026 年改为 many，不再写具体人数。」
