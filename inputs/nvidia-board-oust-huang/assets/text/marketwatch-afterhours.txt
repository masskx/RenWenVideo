Nasdaq futures fall as Nvidia sinks 8% after hours on report board may oust Huang
Shares of AMD and Broadcom rise after hours, cushioning the Nasdaq-100 as most other chip stocks fall
Updated
Nov. 11, 2026, 11:41 p.m. ET
Share
Resize
(2 min)
Stock futures traded lower late Wednesday.
Photo:
MarketWatch photo illustration
/
iStockphoto
Nasdaq-100 futures
NQ00
fell nearly 1% on Wednesday evening after Nvidia shares dropped about 8% in after-hours trading, following a report from the Wall Street Journal that
the chipmaker’s board was preparing to oust CEO Jensen Huang
. Nvidia shares
NVDA
had risen 2.9% during Wednesday’s regular session.
Shares of Advanced Micro Devices
AMD
and Broadcom
AVGO
rose by about 4% and 3%, respectively, after hours. S&P 500 futures
ES00
fell 0.3%, while Dow futures
YM00
rose slightly.
