Nasdaq futures fall as Nvidia sinks 8% after hours on report board may oust Huang

Nasdaq-100 futures NQ00 fell nearly 1% on Wednesday evening after Nvidia shares dropped about 8% in after-hours trading, following a report from the Wall Street Journal that the chipmaker’s board was preparing to oust CEO Jensen Huang . Nvidia shares NVDA had risen 2.9% during Wednesday’s regular session.

Shares of Advanced Micro Devices AMD and Broadcom AVGO rose by about 4% and 3%, respectively, after hours. S&P 500 futures ES00 fell 0.3%, while Dow futures YM00 rose slightly.
