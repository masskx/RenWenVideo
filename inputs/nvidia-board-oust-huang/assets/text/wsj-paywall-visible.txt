Nvidia Is Preparing to Oust CEO Jensen Huang
Move follows company investigation into an affair with a Chinese woman alleged to be a spy
By
Robbie Whelan
,
Josh Dawsey
and
Amrith Ramkumar
Listen

Jensen Huang

Nvidia’s

board is preparing to oust CEO
Jensen Huang
after he had two extramarital affairs, including one with a suspected Chinese spy and another with an employee.
The board is planning to meet as soon as Wednesday to determine his future, people familiar with the matter said. His departure would bring down the curtain on an era during which he stoked the global artificial-intelligence boom and became a geopolitical star. It would leave one of the world’s most valuable companies in need of a new leader.
