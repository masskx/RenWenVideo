Meet the Startup: Aegiq, A Full-Stack Photonic Quantum Computing Company
Quantum computers have a deployment problem. Aegiq is solving it.
The Sheffield-based startup is building photonic quantum computers designed to connect over standard fiber networks and manufacture using standard semiconductor processes — making quantum hardware practical to deploy at scale, not just in a lab.
In this interview, Aegiq’s team walks through how they’ve integrated NVIDIA technology into their core workflow. Using NVIDIA’s Ising solver, they cut quantum dot calibration time by 3x. With cuTENSOR libraries, they went from 1 million point meshes on existing GPUs to billions — and are targeting 10 trillion point meshes in collaboration with NVIDIA.
The applications are real and near-term: aerospace simulation, fusion energy, materials science, quantum chemistry.
Aegiq joined NVIDIA Inception to accelerate access to customers and compute, and is now one of the startups demonstrating what happens when photonic quantum hardware meets GPU-accelerated simulation.
🌱 Aegiq is a member of NVIDIA Inception, a program supporting startups building at the frontier of AI and deep tech.
